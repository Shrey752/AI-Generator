Scene Report

Victim: Marcus Vane, 44. Owner of The Blue Vesper, a jazz bar on Delancey Street. Found dead in the alley behind the building at 02:15 by a delivery driver.

Cause of death: Single blunt-force blow to the posterior skull. Weapon not found at the scene — recovered later (see Evidence F). The blow was delivered from behind.

Context: The bar closed at 01:30. Last call was 01:15. Vane was last seen alive near the service area at approximately 01:35, after most staff had departed.

Key Fact

The Kill Window — 01:40 to 01:50

Rain began at 01:40. The body was fully soaked. The victim died after the rain started. This anchors everything: the killer was in the alley between 01:40 and 01:50.

Weapon: Narrow, heavy, cylindrical object — consistent with an umbrella's weighted brass handle (see Evidence F).
